Every building is drawn at the same scale and from the same camera: an isometric 3/4 top-down view from the front-left, like Township or SimCity BuildIt, and stands on its own small diamond-shaped isometric base tile of light grey pavement with a thin kerb.

Style: premium hand-painted 3D cartoon, chunky simplified shapes, bold saturated but harmonious colours, soft light from the top left, clean crisp edges, a thin dark navy outline around each building and its base. Bright, clean, freshly rebuilt and lively: fresh paint, sparkling windows, potted plants, small trees, fabric awnings, string lights, benches and little details. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos or readable signs anywhere; use picture signs only. Do not use magenta or pink anywhere in the buildings. Everything is fully opaque: glass is painted opaque light blue, steam and smoke are solid cartoon puffs, and nothing lets the background show through. No people, no cars except where listed.
