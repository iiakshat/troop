## What this is

**Siren Squad** (package `com.iiakshat.sirensquad`, studio mark "axnex"; the repo, the Godot save folder `app_userdata/Troop` and many file names keep the old working title). A 2.5D arithmetic army game for Android (portrait, offline-first). The squad stays put at the bottom of a city street and slides between 3 lanes. Pickups, gangs, obstacles and red gates come towards it. The squad fires all the time: one bullet or knife per troop. Between levels the player rebuilds a ruined city.

- **Hero:** an ordinary guy in casual clothes who always throws knives. **Tapping** the lower half of the screen heats him up: idle he throws at 0.4 of his rate, steady tapping (about 4/s) holds full rate (`units.json hero.tap`); a swipe steers and never fires (`TouchClassifier`). A PISTOL pickup gives him a gun for that level.
- **Armory (v2):** a weapon track for every weapon (squad ranks and the four Bottle Shot weapons), 4 tiers × 5 steps; steps change damage and rate, never counts (`config/armory.json`). Squad Power, the lobby's lightning number, is the HUD's live firepower at level start.
- **Powers (v2):** one free shield and one free bomb, each recharging 5 h after use (clock guarded, an ad refills); stored charges come on top. The bomb is thrown at any moment of a level and lands in an arc.
- **Allies (ranks, unlock by level):** Constable (pistol), Sergeant (shotgun, L4), Inspector (revolver, pierces armour, L7), SWAT (rifle burst, L13), Sniper (L21), Commando (launcher splash, L31). Numbers in `config/units.json`; the Codex shows each as "= N constables".
- **Enemies:** Thugs, Knifers, Gunmen, Brutes (armour halves damage), riot, biker, Molotov (firebombs a lane), sniper_foe (laser, then a kill), Grenadier, and a boss per district (walks in shooting, big HP bar).
- **Gate rows:** offers (+N, ×k, recruits, weapons, powers, foe mods) in 1-2 lanes only; the others hold obstacles (pothole, barricade, red gate, car, barrel). A rare "lucky row" has offers in all 3.
- **Powers:** SHIELD and BOMB charges (v2: free ones on a 5 h recharge plus stored ones); the head-start booster is taken in before a level.
- **End of level:** the squad bursts into the gang's stash (one set per district) for the Bottle Shot finale with the district's weapon (1 knives, 2 pistol, 3 bow, 4 throwing stars, 5 sniper); hits in a row build a combo; then 1-3 stars from the share of the peak squad still standing.
- **Meta (v1):** a city map of 5 districts × 10 levels. Each district boss (L10/20/30/40/50) liberates it. 25 buildings are rebuilt with coins (build timers) and earn coins per hour into capped storage. Police Station upgrades (10 levels per room, gated by district). Daily 7-day calendar, 3 daily missions, lucky spin, star chests, Codex.
- **Money:** ads only. v2: real AdMob (rewarded + interstitial, Google's **test IDs** until the owner's AdMob app exists, `config/ads.json`) behind `Ads`; when no rewarded ad is ready the old placeholder countdown stands in. Analytics: Firebase for adults only, a local JSON log always. A neutral birth-year screen decides child or adult before any SDK starts (child: under 13, or under 18 in India; child-directed G-rated non-personalised ads, Firebase never started). PC and debug runs use the placeholder provider.

## Commands

- Play on PC: double-click `play.bat` (arrows or A/D or swipe to change lane, S = shield, B = bomb). `play.bat -- lobby=1` opens the map; `-- coins=2000` unlocks everything for testing.
- Android APK: `build_apk.bat` runs the tests, prepares the **Gradle** build template (`tools/android_template.py` into the git-ignored `android/build/`; the first build downloads Gradle and about 1 GB of libraries into `%USERPROFILE%\.gradle`), exports `build/troop-debug.apk` (Siren Squad, `com.iiakshat.sirensquad`, about 127 MB with AdMob + Firebase) at low CPU priority, and installs it on a USB-connected phone if there is one (`build_apk.bat noinstall` skips that). One-time setup: `python -I tools/setup_android.py` (adb, apksigner, JDK 17, platform 36, build-tools 36.1.0 into `../_tools`, the Godot Android templates and a debug keystore). `google-services.json` (owner, from Firebase) goes in the project root and is never committed. The owner's AdMob/Firebase steps: `docs/owner_ads_setup.md`; privacy docs: `docs/PRIVACY.md`, `docs/play_data_safety.md`, `docs/families_checklist.md`. No release (signed) preset yet.
- Art with Gemini (shared key, keep requests few, many assets per sheet): `python -I tools/gemini_image.py <out.png> <aspect> <prompt.txt> [--ref img]`; sheets live in `art_src/gen/`, then `tools/slice_env.py` / `tools/slice_icons.py` / `tools/slice_city.py` / `tools/slice_brand.py` (key art: loading screens, launcher icon layers, Play icon and feature graphic from `art_src/keyart/`) / `tools/make_hand.py` (the pointing hand).
- Sounds: `python -I tools/make_sfx.py . siren_whoop siren_chirp` re-synthesises the brand sirens.
- Levels: `python -I tools/gen_levels.py` regenerates `config/levels/level_1..50.json`.
- Re-bake characters after editing `config/characters.json`: `$G --headless --path . --script res://tools/bake_vat.gd [-- only=hero]`; preview: `$G --path . res://tools/preview_chars.tscn -- clip=run`.
- Previews: `tools/preview_ui.gd` (any retention panel), `tools/preview_world.tscn` (district themes), `tools/preview_finale.tscn` (Bottle Shot).
- Godot: `../_tools/godot/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe` (call it `$G`).
- Import: `$G --headless --path . --import` (also after adding a new `class_name` file).
- Tests: `$G --headless --path . --script res://tests/run_tests.gd`
- Economy model: `$G --headless --path . --script res://tests/econ_sim.gd` prints the purchase curve, the **no-ad pace** (the upgrades a no-ad player brings into each level) with the `no_ad_pace_reaches_every_level` check, and writes `_local/pace.json`.
- Tuning report: `$G --headless --path . --script res://tests/tune.gd -- [level=N | levels=A-B | list=a,b] profile=paced [zero=1] [tap_rate=N] [powers=0|1] [out=x.json]` (run 6 processes over level ranges at below-normal priority). `profile=paced` is the v2 calibration profile: each level gets the no-ad pace's upgrades (from `_local/pace.json`, else the model is run), 4 taps/s, no shield or bomb. `checks=1` prints per level the stored route for a tapper, a non-tapper and (L1) a light tapper, and the best route with zero upgrades.
- Level calibration (after any change to `units.json`, `level_sim.gd`, the economy pace or the generator): `tune.gd calibrate=1 profile=paced ratio=0 out=...` per range, then `python -I tools/gen_levels.py --merge <outs>`, repeat while k factors are far from 1, then a plain `tune.gd profile=paced out=...` run (this records the winning `route`), then `--merge` again, then `checks=1`. `config/levels/calibration.json` holds each level's scale and stored routes; uncalibrated levels regenerate byte-identically. Levels 1-3 open with a `fixed` hero-alone gang (`gen_levels.py OPENING`) that calibration never rescales.
- Debug args after `--` (see the top of `src/main.gd`): `level=N autoplay=1 lanes=2,1,1 strike=1 boosters=headstart,bomb report=1 revive=1|auto stop_at=win|loss|revive frames=N screenshot=path shots=100,200 out=prefix lobby=1 reset=1 coins=N save=path.json ads=1 tutor=auto perf=1 boot=1|0 age=1 shield_at=N panel=station|armory|style|settings|daily|missions|spin|codex|booster|chest|welcome|revive|liberation|map|ad|interstitial`. `perf=1` logs every frame to `_local/perf.log` and prints the worst level frames after the first 2 s; `boot=1` plays the brand boot screens (intro, then the key-art loading screen; debug-save runs alternate the shot in `user://boot_art_debug.cfg`) even with debug args (they play only on a plain launch otherwise); `age=1` forces the age screen; `shield_at=N` presses the shield at frame N (with a charge if the squad has none). `save=` plays on a copy of a JSON save and never writes. Any run with autoplay, level, coins, reset, stop_at, frames, shots, screenshot, chain, boosters or district plays on a throwaway copy of the real save and never writes it (`write=1` persists on purpose, `real_reset=1` wipes the real save); autoplay/save runs log analytics to `user://events_debug.log`. For screenshots add `--always-on-top --position 0,0 --fixed-fps 60 --resolution 540x960`. Autoplay ignores mouse and touch and skips boosters, revive and ads.
- Delete `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Troop\save.json` to test as a new player (a new player skips the menu). Agents: still pass `save=_local/x.json` (a missing file = blank player, never written) so runs do not depend on the owner's progress. The save is written atomically (`save.json.tmp`, then renamed; the previous file stays as `save.json.bak` and is loaded if `save.json` is broken).

## Rules for this game

- Every number lives in `config/`. After changing a level, run `tune.gd profile=paced` and keep `test_levels_are_winnable_and_reward_planning` passing (the paced profile wins every stored route without powers; a non-tapper wins from L6 and loses L2-L3; L1 falls to light tapping).
- Upgrades gate progress fairly (v2): levels assume the no-ad pace from `econ_sim`; any economy change moves the pace, so re-run the calibration after it.
- No `Engine.time_scale` change and no pause during a level, except first-run tutorial steps (Bottle Shot keeps its slow motion).
- The arithmetic must stay exact (see DECISIONS.md). Never fake an outcome.
- No words on buttons or game objects (owner). Icons and numbers only; exceptions are listed in the spec §0.
- Engagement without deception: no fake countdowns, disguised or unclosable ads, confirm-shaming, or misleading odds (spec §0).
- Original assets only; log anything new in `ASSETS.md`; CC BY items also go into `SettingsPanel.CREDITS`.
- Git: local config is iiakshat / jakshat569@gmail.com. Remote `iiakshat/troop` (public). Work on `dev` only; after a green local `build_apk.bat`, fast-forward `main` to `dev` and push both. Push with the token in `.env` via a per-command auth header.
