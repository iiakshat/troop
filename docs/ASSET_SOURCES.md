=== FIND CITY ENVIRONMENT + PROPS + WEAPONS (low-poly, CC0) 
TOP: Use the Kenney city stack: City Kit Commercial + City Kit Roads + Car Kit, plus City Kit Industrial for the hideout. All four are one author and one palette-texture style (512px colormap per kit), so the city looks cohesive and batches cheaply. Add the Kenney Food Kit for the bottle/can minigame (it has crushed/broken swap models for hits), the CC0 Flat Shaded Melee pack for bats/knives/batons (100-300 tris, one 8x8 palette), and the Quaternius Ultimate Gun Pack for pistol/revolver/rifle, decimated before use. Cherry-pick the hydrant, bench and trash bins from KayKit City Builder Bits only if the style difference is acceptable. 
NOTES: The sandbox couldn't reach several hosts.
- kenney.nl, quaternius.com, poly.pizza, static.poly.pizza and *.itch.io all failed from this sandbox (schannel SEC_E_INVALID_TOKEN / connection failure). A request to run outside the sandbox was denied. So I could not resolve the official kenney.nl/media/pages/... zip URLs or check poly.pizza.
- I used the official uploads on OpenGameArt instead (posted by the 'Kenney' and 'quaternius' accounts) and the official KayKit-Game-Assets GitHub repos. All URLs above were actually fetched with curl and the contents inspected.
- The Kenney zips are identical-version packs (Commercial 2.1, Roads 2.2, Industrial 2.0, Car 3.1). Each has a License.txt stating CC0.

- I did not inspect Quaternius 'Ultimate Food' or 'City' packs, or KayKit Weapons, because their hosts were unreachable. The KayKit Adventurers weapons are fantasy and do not fit.

Texture approach:
- Every Kenney kit uses its own 512x512 colormap.png (about 11 KB). The hashes differ per kit, so expect one material per kit, roughly 5-6 materials total for the whole city.
- KayKit uses one 1024x1024 atlas per pack (about 20 KB).
- The melee pack uses one 8x8 palette.
- The Quaternius guns use flat MTL colours with no texture. Bake them to a palette or set them as Godot StandardMaterial albedo colours.

Budget:
- City props are 20-200 tris.
- Buildings are 0.8-1.9k tris; use the low-detail Kenney buildings (about 190 tris) for far rows.
- Cars are 1.2-2.3k tris, so keep them sparse.
- Guns are 1-1.9k tris and need decimating.

Style caution: Kenney (slim, clean) and KayKit (chunky, toy-like) look different side by side. Choose one family for the street, and match the character pack's style when that is chosen.

Licence bookkeeping for ASSETS.md: the Quaternius gun zip and the Pichuliru melee zip contain no licence file. Cite their OGA pages, both marked CC0.

- police: male-c (a real police officer with cap and badge)
