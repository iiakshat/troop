# Troop v1.1: owner play-test round (2026-11-09)

Builds on `2026-11-08-troop-v1-design.md` (still the contract; same rules: numbers in config, exact arithmetic, icons + numbers not words, no deceptive patterns, ads are placeholder popups). Owner decisions already made: good play stays powerful (planned routes may finish near full squad); troops are drawn smaller than the hero and a step behind him.

## Owner feedback → decisions

1. **"Ranks are still not clear; tell the user when a new rank unlocks."**
   - On the map, when the player reaches a rank's unlock level, show a full-screen **New Rank** card before the level: big character render (or glossy rank icon), the rank's name (one word allowed), dmg, rate, special icon, "= N" value vs a constable, and the recruit gate icon it comes from.
   - In-level, the first time a rank joins: a 1 s spotlight (time slows to 0.3, a ring under the new troops, the RankToast).
   - Codex rows get the same "= N" value line, sorted by value.
2. **"Why did I die when -40% became 0%, and why did it take so long to get to 0?"**
   - Reproduce first. A red percent gate shot up to 0% must be harmless when passed (0% = no change), and past 0 it becomes a troop offer, as in v0.2.
   - Shooting a red gate must visibly tick every few hits. Tune `step` so a fully committed squad clears a -40% gate in about 2-3 s, not 10.
   - If the death had another cause (an obstacle or melee in that lane), fix the cause and make the cause visible.
3. **"Obstacles not destroyed: all followers in that lane die."**
   - A missed obstacle costs exactly the number shown on it (troops or %), never the whole squad.
   - The view must kill exactly that many figures (scaled for the shown-figure cap), never all of them.
   - Every obstacle shows its cost number before you reach it.
4. **"Music and sound have the same icon and neither works."**
   - Separate glossy icons: a music note for music, a speaker for sound, with an "off" state for each.
   - Both toggles must work immediately and persist: sound mutes SFX, music stops or starts the track.
5. **"So many coins but nothing to buy."**
   - The current district's buildings must be buildable before it is liberated (liberation adds a bonus).
   - Building upgrades and station levels must always offer something to buy at roughly the current income.
   - Add a "best next purchase" pointer on the map (bobbing hand) whenever the player can afford something.
   - Keep cosmetics as a coin sink too. Check the curve so a player at level N has 1-3 affordable things after each win, and nothing feels pointless.
6. **"Why don't the Kingpin etc. move? If they don't, we shouldn't look like we're moving."**
   - When a boss (or any gang that stops to shoot) engages, the street stops scrolling: the squad plants its feet and fights (a standoff), and the scroll resumes when the gang dies.
   - Bosses visibly walk toward the squad during the standoff.
   - The sim models this (traveled does not advance during a standoff). Re-run calibration: stored routes must still win, and the spec §3 win-rate curve holds.
7. **"The bottle level should get harder as we grow, and show counts and combos."**
   - Difficulty grows with level inside each district: more bottles, more motion, smaller or farther targets, decoys, and fewer spare knives later.
   - Combo counter: consecutive hits raise a combo multiplier, shown as a big number with a flame icon. A miss resets it. Coins use bottles × combo.
   - The HUD shows bottles left and knives left as icons with numbers.
8. **"The character should have a knife-throwing animation."**
   - Bake a real overhand **throw** clip for the hero.
   - Quaternius has no throw clip, so build one procedurally: keyframe the shoulder, elbow and wrist bones of the rig into an Animation, then bake it to VAT like the other clips.
   - Use it for in-level knife throws and in Bottle Shot.
9. **"The end building doesn't fit bottles and knives; reaching a building and throwing knives feels irrelevant."**
   - Make the finale a story beat: the gang's **stash**. The level ends at the gang's bar or hideout, the squad bursts in, and the hero smashes their bottle stash.
   - Each district has a matching set: 1 back-alley bar, 2 market carnival stall, 3 docks crate stack, 4 factory conveyor, 5 rooftop neon lounge.
   - The approach shows that place (sign without words, neon bottle silhouette), not a generic HQ.
10. **"Each new stage (every 10 levels) should change the game or weapon: archer, pistol at bottles, harder control."**
    - Bottle Shot gets a weapon per district, each with its own feel and control. Same rewards and logic API; per-weapon config.
      - **1 knives:** drag, aim, release; arc; slow-mo chase.
      - **2 pistol:** tap to shoot, sway, recoil kick, limited bullets, fast targets.
      - **3 bow:** hold to draw (power), release; gravity drop; wind indicator.
      - **4 throwing stars:** swipe direction and speed; ricochet off metal.
      - **5 sniper rifle:** scope zoom, breathing sway, hold to steady, one shot per target.
11. **"Teach first-timers the bottle game and the normal game, stopping and teaching."**
    - First run of level 1: freeze-frame tutorials with a pointing hand, no words, each shown once ever (`seen_hints`):
      - drag to steer, demonstrated, game paused until the player drags
      - the first gate choice: highlight the good lane
      - shooting a red gate up
      - the shield and bomb buttons, the first time a gang arrives
    - First Bottle Shot: a ghost hand demonstrates drag-aim-release, the first throw waits for the player, and each new weapon gets a one-time demo in its own district.
