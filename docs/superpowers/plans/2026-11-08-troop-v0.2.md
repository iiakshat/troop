# Troop v0.2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** executed natively in-session (subagent budget exhausted until reset). Steps use checkbox syntax. Commit to `dev` after each task; push = CI APK build + auto fast-forward of `main`.

**Goal:** Turn the v0.1 prototype into a deeper, better-looking 5-level game: honest melee, long bullet streams, new ranks/enemies/pickups/obstacles, real 3D characters and city, the Bottle Shot finale, a police-station base with cosmetics, real SFX/music, bright-cartoon icon UI, one-time hints.

**Architecture:** Keep the pure `LevelSim` (tested, brute-force tuned) as the source of truth; the view only renders it. Characters are Quaternius CC0 models baked offline into vertex-animation textures (VAT) so crowds stay one MultiMesh per role. Environment from Kenney CC0 kits. Finale and station are separate scenes/modules with pure logic cores.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7.2 (GL Compatibility), GDScript, GitHub Actions (public repo).

**Spec:** plan page https://claude.ai/artifact/Ks3CsWzW6F6jYwUbXeQeoU (approved 2026-11-08) + owner messages; research summary in `docs/ASSET_SOURCES.md`.

## Global Constraints

- UI: bright cartoon; **icons, not words** on buttons, powers, pickups; numbers only on tags; no floating captions.
- Coins buy only: police station rooms + cosmetics. Ranks unlock by level progress.
- Finale: exactly 3 knives; slow-mo zoomed knife cam, normal speed at impact; glass shatters; broken bottles boost coins.
- 5 levels (Downtown), boss on level 5; new idea per level (L1 recruit/×2, L2 Inspector/armour, L3 gunmen/shield/barrels, L4 SWAT/riot/bikers, L5 boss).
- Hints: each mechanic once ever, icon/hand cue; none after level 2.
- Assets CC0 (game-icons.net CC BY 3.0 → credits screen + ASSETS.md; Luckiest Guy Apache-2.0).
- Low-end Android: ≤ ~600k tris on screen; one draw call per crowd role.

## Review Focus

1. Gang wiped to zero mid-melee: melee stops, no phantom losses (test in sim).
2. Bomb used when nothing in range: refused, charge kept (existing test).
3. Turncoat on a gang already partly dead: converts min(n, alive) only.
4. Player at 1 troop (hero only) meets riot shields: dies only on contact, not at range.
5. Save from v0.1 (upgrades dict, no station) loads without crash and maps to station rooms.

### Task 1: Sim — honest front, row melee, long range
Files: `src/core/level_sim.gd`, `config/units.json`, `tests/test_level_sim.gd`
- Horde gains `cols`, `row_gap`; front z = `z_base - floor(killed_from_front/cols)*row_gap`; contact only when front ≥ contact_z.
- Melee: at contact, one row per `melee_tick` (0.25 s) trades `min(row_alive*power, troops)`; emits `melee` events `{horde, n_enemy, lost}`; continues until horde or squad gone.
- `fire_range` 45; troops still target nearest horde in range.
- Tests: `test_contact_only_when_front_reaches`, `test_melee_trades_row_by_row`, `test_bigger_gang_kills_squad`.

### Task 2: Sim — content
Files: same + `config/units.json`
- SWAT rank: dmg 2, rate 2.5, `pierce_count` 2 (each hit kills up to 2 hp-1 units).
- Enemies: `riot` (front shield hp 6 absorbs damage first unless attacker pierces; power 2), `biker` (speed 9, power 3, hp 2), `boss` (hp 400, armour 1, power 999, stops at 14 m and smashes 3 troops/2 s).
- Gates: `turncoat` (k units of next gang join as constables), weapon `smg` (dmg 1 rate 8).
- Obstacles: `barrel` (hp 4; when destroyed kills enemies within 6 m of its z), `car` (hp 25, lane block, costs dmg troops), `barricade`.
- Tests per item.
